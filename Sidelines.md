---
title: "Sidelines"
tags: ["poetry"]
layout: note.njk
---
I loved and hated  
dared and feared;  
the fear's always won.  

In always choosing  
self-defense,  
I never chose anyone. 

And so love lay  
inside my heart,  
locked-up and on trial

with tally marks on  
prison walls  
holding onto denial.

And now that courage  
and experience  
have let me afford bail,  

the love is free but  
on parole;  
restless, naive, frail.

With how much my  
defense was helped,  
no-one would have believed
  
that locked up love   
kept meaning mostly  
shallow love was recieved.

Not only does   
my love now yearn  
to love unbridled and free,

but also be cradled   
by the the gaze   
of someone willing to see...

To give the love that's missing...  

To catch the overflow...  

To fill up all the darkness...  

By reminding me to glow...  

Though my love knows not it's weakness,  
it knows not also it's strength;  
crushing flowers instead of just  
admiring at arms-length.  

But I refuse to fault my love.  
for the only way it shines  
is by daring to escape  
the prison of sidelines.  

_-Abdullah Alam_
