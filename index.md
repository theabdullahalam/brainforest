## about
A digital garden is a personal website that is a collection of notes and pages linking to one another, as opposed to a chronological feed. All the pages have links to many other pages, and the reader is expected to explore the website organically.

I have built my website around that idea, but with a slightly different metaphor: a forest. A forest grows organically outward in whatever space is available. 

I have redesigned my website to just be a space where I keep sharing things without worrying about structure or links too much: only adding structure as and when necessary. 

Feel free to wander and look around.
